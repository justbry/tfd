Questions

What's a ledger event?

One thing that happened to your money: a payment, a refund, a credit used, a booking. A typical family or member makes about 7 a month, so Free's 300 events covers about 40 families. Past that, Starter. After that, most organizations upgrade for features, not limits.

What happens if I go over my limit?

Nothing stops and nothing is billed. Event limits are soft on Free, Starter, Pro and Audited: your books keep running, and if you stay over for two months we'll suggest a plan that fits. There is no metered overage. Only Platform pays per event, about $1 per 10,000.

Who qualifies for non-profit & co-op pricing?

US 501(c) organizations (we check your IRS determination letter or EIN), and cooperatives (we check your articles or bylaws). Non-profits with gross receipts of $50,000 or less get the witness free on the Free plan once it ships: up to about 66 countersigned receipts a month (Free's 200 credits at 3 each).

Do the annual and non-profit discounts stack?

Yes. Annual billing is ten months' price for twelve months, applied to whichever price you qualify for.

What will "Audited" mean? Is it an audit?

It isn't an audit; it makes your books audit-ready. Once it ships, Trifold will countersign each receipt and monthly seal and log it publicly, which proves Trifold saw that exact record at that time and that nobody changed it afterwards. It doesn't prove the money moved or that the books are complete. That makes your auditor's job faster, not unnecessary.

Can I self-host for free?

Yes. The whole engine and the tfd CLI are free to run yourself, including receipts, seals, reconcile and simulate. tfd is source-available under the Functional Source License. The license lets you use and change it for your own organization, for non-commercial education and research, and for professional services to a licensee; it doesn't allow selling it as a competing hosted service. Every tfd release becomes Apache-2.0 two years after it ships. The verifier and receipt format are Apache-2.0 today.

Does Trifold file my taxes?

No. Trifold keeps provable books and prepares the reports your CPA or enrolled agent needs. Partner bookkeepers can close the month for you.
