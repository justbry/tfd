## Three things a non-profit's books have to answer

### 1. Donors and members who want to see where the money went

**How Trifold answers it** (chapter 03, Proof): every gift and every due paid through Stripe gets a receipt signed with your organization's key. A donor or a co-op member can check theirs on the verify page, without an account.

### 2. A board that needs the month, not a spreadsheet

**How Trifold answers it** (chapters 02 and 03, Books and Proof): payments post as balanced, append-only books, and you seal each month before the board meets. Anyone holding the seal can check that the month hasn't changed since.

### 3. A grant report or an audit

**How Trifold answers it:** every number traces to the Stripe payment behind it. Your auditor gets the events in open formats (hledger, Parquet, CSV) and can rebuild the books themselves. Tracking money by grant or fund isn't built: the books show what came in through Stripe, not which grant it belongs to.
