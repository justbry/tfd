## Three things that weigh on a church treasurer

### 1. Giving records the treasurer trusts

Monthly and yearly gifts at each giving level, one-time gifts and the roof campaign arrive through Stripe, then end up in a spreadsheet one person understands.

**How Trifold answers it** (chapters 01 and 02, Money in and Books): every gift lands as an event and posts as balanced books, automatically. Corrections are new events, never edits, so the record of what was given doesn't quietly change.

### 2. A monthly report to the board

**How Trifold answers it** (chapter 03, Proof): seal each month before the board meets. A seal is a signed fingerprint of the month's gifts; anyone holding it can check that the month hasn't changed since.

### 3. Givers asking for their year's total

**How Trifold answers it:** today, every gift has a receipt signed with your church's key, and a giver can check it on the verify page without an account. Your books export to hledger, Parquet and CSV for your accountant. Year-end giving statements, one per giver, are on the roadmap, marked Later.
