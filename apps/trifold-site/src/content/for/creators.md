## Three things that eat a creator's launch

### 1. Launch-month chaos

The founding cohort joins in one month, and many buy a course or a download at sign-up.

**How Trifold answers it** (chapters 01 and 02, Money in and Books): every Stripe payment lands as an event and posts as balanced books, automatically. Duplicate deliveries from Stripe are recorded once.

### 2. Refunds

**How Trifold answers it:** a refund is a new event, never an edit, so the month shows what came in and what went back. Each refund has its own signed receipt (chapter 03, Proof).

### 3. Knowing what actually came in

**How Trifold answers it:** gross collected, Stripe fees, refunds and payouts come from the same events, and every number traces to its payment. Seal the month and any later change shows.

An honest note: in today's simulation, a course sale is booked with the member's tier, not as a separate income line.
