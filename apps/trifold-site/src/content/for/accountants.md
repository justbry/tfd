## Three things clients usually hand you

### 1. A year of payments with no trail

**Trifold:** every number traces to a Stripe event, and every month can be sealed. A seal is a signed fingerprint of the month's events; if anything in a sealed month changes, it stops matching.

### 2. Books rebuilt from bank exports

**Trifold:** the books are built from the payment events themselves, append-only, and reconciled against Stripe's records on request (paid invoices, refunds, disputes, payouts, fees).

### 3. Receipts nobody can check

**Trifold:** each payment has a receipt signed with the client's key. You can verify any receipt or seal with the free command-line tool or on the verify page.
